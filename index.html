FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to install anything?

No. Pro Slide runs entirely in your web browser. Open it, sign in if you want cloud sync, and start building.

Where are my presentations stored?

They are saved in your browser's local storage first. If you sign in with Google, they are also mirrored to a dedicated folder in your own Google Drive through our Apps Script backend, so you can open them on other devices.

Can I open my slides in PowerPoint?

Yes. Export to .pptx at any time. Text, shapes, images, icons, charts, tables, equations, ink, speaker notes and backgrounds are included. Some advanced effects, such as the custom 3D shape shading and entrance animations, are simplified or not carried into the .pptx file.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Local-only decks can be lost, so we recommend signing in for Drive sync or downloading .json backups regularly from the dashboard menu.

How does upgrading to Pro work?

Choose a monthly or yearly plan, pay with UPI / GPay by scanning the in-app QR code, then tap "I've paid". Your payment is verified manually and Pro is unlocked on your Google account, typically within a day.

Does it work on mobile?

You can browse your presentations and present from any modern device. The full editor is optimized for desktop and tablet screens with a mouse, trackpad or pen.
